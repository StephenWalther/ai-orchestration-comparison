# Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement

## 1. Parties and Effective Date

This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement ("Agreement") is effective January 15, 2025 ("Effective Date"), between Asterquill Analytics, Inc., a Delaware corporation ("Asterquill"), and Brindlewave Systems, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company ("Brindlewave"). Each is a "Party," and together they are the "Parties." A Party providing Confidential Information is the "Disclosing Party"; a Party receiving it is the "Receiving Party."

## 2. Purpose

The Parties wish to evaluate a collaboration on an inventory forecasting platform, including technical integration and commercial terms ("Purpose"). This Agreement permits evaluation only. Production deployment and any commercial engagement require a separate written agreement.

## 3. Confidential Information and Exclusions

"Confidential Information" means nonpublic information disclosed by or on behalf of a Disclosing Party to a Receiving Party on or after the Effective Date and during this Agreement, in any form, that is marked confidential or reasonably should be understood as confidential from its nature and the circumstances. It includes software, designs, technical documentation, pricing, business plans, financial information, and the substance of the Parties' negotiations. Copies, summaries, and analyses containing or revealing such information are also Confidential Information.

Confidential Information excludes information the Receiving Party can demonstrate through written records: (a) was lawfully known to it without a confidentiality duty before disclosure; (b) becomes publicly available without a breach of this Agreement; (c) is lawfully received from a third party without a confidentiality duty; or (d) is independently developed without use of or reference to the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information. A combination is not excluded merely because individual elements are publicly known.

## 4. Permitted Use, Access, and Safeguards

The Receiving Party shall use Confidential Information solely for the Purpose and disclose it only to its employees, officers, contractors, and professional advisers who need access for that Purpose ("Representatives"). Before receiving access, Representatives must be bound by written confidentiality and restricted-use obligations at least as protective as this Agreement, or professional duties of confidentiality. The Receiving Party is responsible for its Representatives' compliance.

The Receiving Party shall protect Confidential Information with reasonable care and no less care than it uses for its own similarly sensitive information. It shall limit copying to what is reasonably necessary for the Purpose, maintain confidentiality notices, and promptly notify the Disclosing Party upon discovering unauthorized access, use, or disclosure. The Parties shall reasonably cooperate to contain the incident and prevent recurrence.

## 5. Compelled Disclosure

If disclosure is required by law, regulation, or a binding court or governmental order, the Receiving Party may disclose only the portion legally required. To the extent legally permitted, it shall promptly give written notice so the Disclosing Party may seek protection, and provide reasonable assistance at the Disclosing Party's expense. The Receiving Party shall request confidential treatment where available. Required disclosure does not otherwise release its obligations under this Agreement.

## 6. Duration and Survival

This Agreement continues for two years from the Effective Date unless either Party terminates it earlier on thirty days' written notice. Upon expiration or termination, the Receiving Party shall cease using Confidential Information for the Purpose. Confidentiality and restricted-use obligations continue for three years after expiration or termination, except that trade secrets remain protected for as long as they qualify as trade secrets under applicable law. Section 7 specifies additional protection for retained information. Sections 8 through 11 survive as necessary to give effect to their terms.

## 7. Return, Deletion, and Retention

Within thirty days after the Disclosing Party's written request or expiration or termination of this Agreement, the Receiving Party shall return or delete Confidential Information, including copies held by Representatives, and confirm completion in writing upon request. It may retain one archival copy solely for legal compliance or documenting its obligations, and copies required by law or preserved under a legal hold.

Routine backup copies need not be individually removed if they are inaccessible in ordinary operations and deleted through the Receiving Party's normal backup rotation. Retained information may be accessed only for the permitted retention purpose or necessary disaster recovery, remains subject to this Agreement's confidentiality and restricted-use obligations until deleted, and shall not be used to resume the Purpose. Restored backup information shall be deleted when no longer needed for recovery.

## 8. Ownership and No License

Each Disclosing Party retains ownership of its Confidential Information. No patent, copyright, trademark, or other intellectual property license is granted, except the limited permission to use information for the Purpose. Neither Party may reverse engineer, decompile, or disassemble software or prototypes supplied under this Agreement without the Disclosing Party's written consent.

## 9. Evaluation and Relationship

Information is provided "as is," without a promise of accuracy or completeness. Neither Party must disclose particular information or proceed with a transaction. Each bears its own evaluation costs. This Agreement creates no partnership, agency, exclusivity, or commitment to future work. Neither Party may publicly announce the discussions or use the other's name in publicity without written consent.

## 10. Governing Law and Disputes

Delaware law governs this Agreement, excluding its conflict-of-laws rules. The Parties consent to the exclusive jurisdiction of state and federal courts located in Delaware for disputes arising from this Agreement. Either Party may request injunctive relief for unauthorized use or disclosure, subject to the court's determination, in addition to other available remedies.

## 11. General Terms and Execution

Notices must be in writing and delivered to the other Party's designated business contact, with receipt acknowledged in writing. This Agreement is the entire agreement concerning its subject and may be amended only by a writing signed by both Parties. Neither Party may assign it without the other's written consent. Failure to enforce a provision is not a waiver. If a provision is held unenforceable, the remaining provisions continue. Counterparts and electronic signatures may be used.
